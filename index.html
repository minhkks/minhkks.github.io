Research Assistant, BreathAI Lab, Nanyang Technological University

I am a Research Assistant at the BreathAI Lab, Nanyang Technological University (NTU), working with Prof. Wei Lu.

My research looks at what large language models represent inside their hidden states, and at fine-tuning models after pre-training to make them reason better.

I received my Bachelor's degree in Applied Mathematics and Informatics from Hanoi University of Science and Technology (2019–2023), graduating as valedictorian of the Talented Program.
